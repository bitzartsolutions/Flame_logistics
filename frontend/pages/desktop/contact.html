Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our services, onboarding process, and specialized handling capabilities.

How quickly can I get a custom shipping quote? expand_more
For standard land transportation across the GCC, our team provides competitive quotes within couple of minutes. For complex industrial crating or large-scale project logistics, a dedicated specialist will provide a detailed proposal within 2–4 business hours depends upon the volume, requirements etc..
Which regions do you cover for land transportation? expand_more
We provide comprehensive full-truckload (FTL) and less-than-truckload (LTL) land transportation services connecting all major routes across Saudi Arabia and the entire GCC region. Also we cover Land Transportation services to Jordan and Egypt.
Do you offer container transportation services between ports and facilities? expand_more
Yes, we provide reliable container drayage and transport services, seamlessly moving containers between ports, manufacturing hubs, industrial sites, and warehouses.
Where are your warehousing facilities located, and what options do you offer? expand_more
We operate modern, strategically located warehousing facilities in Riyadh and Jeddah featuring Warehouse Management System (WMS) integration, inventory management, and climate-controlled storage zones.
Do you handle customs clearance and cross-border documentation? expand_more
Yes, Flame Logistics manages full customs brokerage services, border clearance, and compliance documentation. We specialize in navigating Saudi Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) regulations and GCC border protocols.
What terminal services do you provide? expand_more
We manage comprehensive terminal logistics, including cargo handling, cross-docking, temporary storage, and loading/unloading operations at key transit nodes.
What is included in your Industrial Crating services? expand_more
We build customised, heavy-duty wooden crates and specialised packaging designed to secure fragile, high-value, or oversized machinery for safe land transport, air freight, Sea Freight and long-term storage.
What does your Packing & Moving service cover? expand_more
We offer complete commercial relocation and residential packing services. Our team handles everything from professional packing, loading, and transport to unpacking and setup at your new site.
