The Journey of Flame

2011

Foundation

Started operations in Riyadh as an establishment with a dedicated team of three professionals and the company's first truck.

A strong commitment to dependable logistics and customer-focused service laid the foundation for future growth.

2012

Fleet Expansion

Expanded the transportation fleet by adding two additional trucks.

This investment increased delivery capacity and strengthened the company's ability to serve more clients efficiently.

2013

Service Diversification

Established the first warehouse in Riyadh and launched the Packing & Relocation Division.

The company expanded beyond transportation to provide comprehensive warehousing and relocation solutions.

2018

Warehouse Expansion

Opened the second warehouse in Riyadh to support growing customer demand.

The additional facility enhanced storage capabilities and improved operational efficiency.

2020

Operational Growth

Expanded the workforce to over 20 employees, increased the fleet to more than eight vehicles, and inaugurated the third warehouse in Riyadh.

These milestones marked a significant step in strengthening logistics capacity and service excellence.

2024

Corporate Transformation

Transitioned from an establishment to a registered company.

This milestone reflected the organization's growth, strengthened its corporate identity, and positioned it for continued expansion.

2025

Nationwide Expansion

Opened the Jeddah branch, expanded the team to more than 40 professionals across Saudi Arabia, established the fourth warehouse in Riyadh, and launched a dedicated logistics yard in Jeddah.

This expansion significantly enhanced nationwide coverage, operational capabilities, and the company's ability to deliver comprehensive logistics solutions across the Kingdom.
